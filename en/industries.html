Application areas

Industry categories come from the legacy site. Descriptions below are planning examples, not sector certification claims. Verify before publishing

Automotive & mobility

Prototype brackets, housings and interface parts; identify fit-critical dimensions and finishing needs early.

Medical devices

Functional prototypes and fixtures; agree material traceability, finish and documentation requirements before release.

Automation & robotics

Frames, end-effectors and bases; manage hole patterns, datum strategy and repeatability.

Consumer electronics

Cosmetic housings and structural parts; align surface finish samples and assembly interfaces.

Aerospace & model aircraft

Lightweight machined structures; define material, revision control and inspection requirements explicitly.

Instrumentation & security

Enclosures, mounts and small assemblies; combine machining with coordinated finishing where needed.
