Engineering review during quotation. Illustrative — verify

Before release, engineering can review tolerance stack-up, thin walls, internal corners, workholding and the dimensional effect of finishing. Any requested design change requires your written approval.

  • Unnecessarily tight or blanket tolerances
  • Deep cavities, sharp internal corners and thin-wall distortion risk
  • Fit-critical surfaces after anodizing or plating
