Frequently asked questions

Quoting, tolerances, lead times and confidentiality.

What processes do you support?

CNC metal and plastic prototypes, precision metal parts, SLA/SLS printing, vacuum casting, RIM, sheet-metal prototypes, 3D scanning and finishing. See Capabilities for the planning range.

What tolerances can you hold?

The legacy site cites about ±0.02 mm for CNC and ±0.05 mm for plastic prototypes. Mark critical characteristics on the drawing; final capability is confirmed by engineering for the specific material, feature and fixture.

What are typical lead times?

The legacy site cites roughly 3 days for appearance prototypes and 5 days for functional CNC prototypes. Batch work, complex parts and parts requiring finishing are quoted case by case. Flag expedite needs in the RFQ.

What files should I provide for a quote?

Send both 3D (STEP/IGS) and 2D (PDF/DWG) files whenever possible. The 2D drawing should identify material, finish, critical tolerances and revision. A 3D model can support a preliminary estimate.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. The legacy site states that it can sign confidentiality agreements and restrict drawing access to project personnel. Confirm the NDA process before sharing controlled information.

Which reports can ship with the parts?

FAI, dimensional reports, material certificates, certificates of conformity and finish reports can be requested in the RFQ. CNC prototypes can start at one piece; vacuum casting is generally more economical at tens of pieces.
